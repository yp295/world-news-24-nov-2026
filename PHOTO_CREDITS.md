# Photo credits

There are no photographs in this entry, so there is nothing to credit
to a photographer or an agency.

Every picture on the page — the masthead ornaments, the world map, the
numbered pins, and the small illustration on each of the 13 story
cards (tower, gavel, chip, target, seismic trace, trophy, magnifying
glass, rocket, capitol dome, football, cricket bat, aeroplane, flame)
— was drawn as vector shapes in code with Pillow by the builder
script, from nothing but coordinates and colour values. No image file
was imported, traced, or copied from anywhere else.
